Pancake Live Sales Monitor v1.6.0

SCENIC PLAYLIST UPDATE
- Replaces the 1-minute timer with natural full-clip playback.
- Adds 100 unique curated scenic Pexels video sources.
- Morning, sunrise, rivers, lakes, sea, tropical coasts, golden hour, sunset, city twilight, night, winter and cloud views.
- Each video switches only after the HTML5 video ended event fires.
- Fisher-Yates shuffle: no repeated video inside a complete 100-video cycle.
- When the cycle finishes, a new shuffled cycle is created and the first clip is prevented from immediately repeating the previous clip.
- Two-layer crossfade is kept.
- Only current + one next clip are loaded, so the browser does not preload 100 videos at once.
- Failed remote clips are skipped without blanking the current view.
- Timer-based SCENIC_ROTATE_MS is removed.
- Video elements no longer use loop.
- Sales totals, complete-snapshot rules, Vercel account management, per-order popup and scoreboard animation are unchanged from v1.5.x.
