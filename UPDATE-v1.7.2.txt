v1.7.2
======

1. Real positive order amounts are text-only green overlays: no pill, box, border or background.
2. Positive verified sales fall from above and land directly over the right-most digits of the main score.
3. Verified decreases rise from below in red so reductions remain visually distinct.
4. Multiple reconciled real orders can hit rapidly and overlap on the score in the same snapshot.
5. The scoreboard still counts quickly while far away and slows to one-by-one digits near the verified target.
6. Sale audio is now two-stage: a soft 'ting' on entry and a glassy landing chime on impact.
7. Every 60-second season now layers multiple atmosphere effects; there are still no people, animals, vehicles or scene props.
8. Pancake Employee Statistic remains the source of truth. Incomplete snapshots HOLD and animation never invents order amounts.
