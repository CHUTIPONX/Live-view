Pancake Live Sales Monitor v1.7.0 — LIVING WORLD SCENE PLAN
============================================================

RULES
- 24 graphic scenes, no background video.
- One scene lasts exactly 60 seconds.
- Scene index is synchronized from the current minute, so different screens normally see the same scene.
- Crossfade between scenes; no black frame.
- People, animals, vehicles, weather and small objects animate according to the environment.
- The world scene is presentation only. It never changes Pancake sales data.

ACTIVE BEHAVIOUR EXAMPLES
- Hot scenes: person rests, dog pants, cow lowers its head to drink, heat haze moves.
- Rain scenes: umbrella walkers move, cat stays under shelter, rain and wet-road reflection animate.
- Winter scenes: people wear coats, dogs curl up, snow falls, smoke rises from houses.
- Spring scenes: petals/flowers/butterflies move and people walk or take photos.
- Beach/coast scenes: boats drift, gulls fly, water sparkles.
- Savanna/desert scenes: animals walk slowly, dust crosses the ground, heat shimmer appears.
- City scenes: cars, trains, pedestrians, lights and reflections animate without covering the sales total.

SALES ANIMATION
- Verified per-order prices may show together in a batch.
- Price chips are larger and remain visible longer before flying into the main score.
- Main total counts quickly while far away and then slows down to one-by-one final digits.
- The final number is always the verified Pancake Employee Statistic snapshot.
