Pancake Live Sales Monitor v1.7.0

WHAT CHANGED
- Removed all dashboard background video playback.
- Added 24 living world graphic scenes.
- Scene changes every 60 seconds and loops continuously.
- Scene rotation is minute-synchronized across browsers.
- Added active people, animals, vehicles and weather behaviours per scene.
- Added spring, summer, monsoon/rain, autumn, winter, dry, tropical and polar environments around the world.
- Enlarged verified green price popups and kept them visible longer.
- Multiple verified order-price popups can remain on screen together before flying into the main total.
- Main scoreboard still slows down near the verified target.

UNCHANGED
- Pancake Employee Statistic remains the sales source of truth.
- Complete-snapshot-only total rule remains.
- Per-order animation remains verified from real orders only.
- Multi-account/Vercel shared configuration remains.
- Security/CSRF/session protections remain.
